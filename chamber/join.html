NP Membership Benefits

Cost: Free

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $100 per year

  • Standard directory listing with contact details
  • Access to monthly networking events
  • Training invitations for one representative

Cost: $300 per year

  • Two complimentary seats at monthly mixers
  • One social media spotlight each year
  • 20% discount on event sponsorships

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $500 per year

  • Featured home page spotlight opportunities
  • Priority invitations to executive events
  • Newsletter advertising placements
  • Dedicated chamber advocacy consultation
