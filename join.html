NP Membership (Non-Profit)

Cost: Free (No Fee)

Designed for registered non-profit, educational, and community service organizations.

Included Benefits:

  • Basic business directory listing
  • Access to monthly community networking events
  • Inclusion in chamber community announcements
  • Access to chamber online resources & guides

Bronze Membership

Cost: $100 / year

Ideal for startups and small local businesses expanding their presence.

Included Benefits:

  • Standard directory listing with company logo
  • Access to all chamber business networking sessions
  • Invitation to professional development workshops
  • 10% discount on chamber event registration

Silver Membership

Cost: $300 / year

Great for established businesses looking for high visibility and marketing support.

Included Benefits:

  • Enhanced directory listing with top placement
  • Free entrance for 2 team members to all monthly mixers
  • Social media business spotlight twice per year
  • 20% discount on chamber sponsorship opportunities
  • Access to targeted business matching services

Gold Membership

Cost: $500 / year

Premier corporate level for market leaders seeking maximum exposure and influence.

Included Benefits:

  • Featured Home Page Spotlight position & premium directory rank
  • VIP access to all executive summits, galas & roundtables
  • Dedicated monthly social media & newsletter features
  • Custom sponsorship packages & annual sponsor recognition
  • Dedicated chamber account manager & business advocacy
